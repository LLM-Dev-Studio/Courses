# Send the Domain Expert Their Link

An Initiative is not a solo exercise. Once it exists, bring the Domain Expert
in.

## Find the Link

The cockpit shows a Domain Expert link, at
`.../initiative/<id>/respond`. Send it to them however you normally would —
chat, email, or any other channel you already use.

## What the Domain Expert Sees

That view is deliberately narrow. It shows:

- The change request.
- The one question currently waiting for them.

It does not show questions you are still drafting, gate findings, or the
intervention comparison. Those stay on your side of the session, in the
cockpit.

> [!TIP]
> Because both views update live, you can run the session as a live call with
> the Domain Expert answering in real time, or asynchronously over several
> days. Neither party needs to refresh the page manually.

## Practical Steps

1. Copy the Domain Expert link from the cockpit.
2. Send it to your Domain Expert.
3. Confirm they can see the change request and are ready to answer questions.

## Key Takeaway

The Domain Expert link opens a narrow, focused view — the change request and
one waiting question — so they can answer without needing to understand the
whole session.
