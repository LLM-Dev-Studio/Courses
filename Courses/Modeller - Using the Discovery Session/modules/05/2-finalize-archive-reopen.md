# Finalize, Archive, and Reopen

Once Shape is complete and you have reviewed the Shape Gate, you can finalize
the Initiative.

## What Finalizing Does

Finalizing records the Initiative as it stands, including whether it closed
cleanly or with open gate findings you chose to override. It is not a
one-way door.

> [!NOTE]
> You can reopen a finalized Initiative later. Finalizing closes the current
> round of the session — it does not delete the record or make it
> unreachable.

## Checking the Archive

After finalizing, confirm that the cockpit shows the Initiative's archive
period, and that the Facilitator link can still return to the archived
record. From there you can reopen it if new information arrives.

## Practical Steps

1. Confirm Shape is complete and the Shape Gate has been reviewed.
2. Finalize the Initiative from the cockpit.
3. Confirm the archive period is shown.
4. Confirm the Facilitator link still opens the archived record.
5. Reopen the Initiative later if the situation changes.

## Key Takeaway

Finalizing closes the current round of a session without discarding the
record — the same Facilitator link still returns to it, archived or reopened.
