# Module 4 Overview: Gates and Shape

This module covers the two advisory gates and the Shape phase, where you
compare possible responses against what Discover and Frame established.

## Why This Matters

Gates catch gaps before you commit to a response. Shape is where you finally
consider more than the one solution that was originally requested.

## What You Will Cover

- The Discovery Gate: what it checks, and how to override it
- Shaping the response across the fixed intervention taxonomy
- The Shape Gate: what it checks, and mixed responses

## Where to Go

- [Initiatives](https://modeller.wiki/docs/concepts/initiatives) — the gates
  and taxonomy described in full
- [Worked Initiative example: delayed building variations](https://modeller.wiki/docs/guides/building-variation-initiative)
  — a full worked Shape phase across six interventions

## Key Takeaway

Both gates are advisory. They report gaps and concerns, but the Facilitator
always makes the final call.
