# The Shape Gate

The Shape Gate checks the response you have assembled, in the same advisory
way as the Discovery Gate.

## What It Checks

The Shape Gate can surface concerns such as:

- A technology intervention recorded without a rationale.
- "No action" never having been considered at all.

## It Is Advisory Too

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Like the Discovery Gate, the Shape Gate never takes the decision away from
> the Facilitator. It surfaces concerns; you decide how to respond.

## Reviewing Mixed Responses

When an Initiative selects several interventions, check that each one has
its own clear description and rationale. A shared outcome does not mean every
intervention needs the same justification — a process change and a
technology change earn their place in the response for different reasons.

## Practical Steps

1. Evaluate the Shape Gate once your interventions are recorded.
2. Confirm every selected intervention — especially any technology
   intervention — has a clear rationale.
3. Confirm "no action" was genuinely considered, even if not selected.
4. Record an override with a reason for any finding you choose not to act on.

## Key Takeaway

The Shape Gate checks that your selected response is justified and
complete — including that doing nothing was genuinely weighed, not skipped.
