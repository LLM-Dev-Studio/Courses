# The Discovery Gate

The Discovery Gate sits between Frame and Shape. It reports what is still
thin in the Initiative before you start comparing responses.

## What It Checks

The Discovery Gate looks for gaps such as:

- A missing outcome.
- An unevidenced problem.

It runs a set of checks against the structured fields you have filled in
through Discover and Frame.

## It Is Advisory

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Discovery Gate never blocks you. It reports findings, but you can
> record an override with a reason and move on.

If a check remains flagged, look at the structured field it points to before
you decide whether to override it. Sometimes the flag is right, and you need
one more question. Sometimes the gap is acceptable for this Initiative, and
an override with a clear reason is the correct response.

## Practical Steps

1. Evaluate the Discovery Gate once Frame's fields are filled in.
2. For each flagged check, inspect the structured field it refers to.
3. Either send a follow-up question to close the gap, or record an override
   with a reason.
4. Move on to Shape once you are satisfied with the record.

## Key Takeaway

The Discovery Gate surfaces gaps in the record, but never forces your hand —
you decide whether to close a gap or override it with a reason.
