  - prompt: What happens if the Discovery Gate flags a finding?
    options:
      - The Initiative cannot proceed to Shape
      - The Facilitator must send another question immediately
      - The Facilitator can inspect the field, then close the gap or override it with a reason
      - The finding is automatically resolved after 24 hours
    answer: The Facilitator can inspect the field, then close the gap or override it with a reason
    explanation: Both gates are advisory — they report findings but never block the Facilitator.
  - prompt: Which of these is one of the eight fixed intervention categories in Shape?
    options:
      - Marketing
      - Organisation
      - Budget
      - Legal
    answer: Organisation
    explanation: The fixed taxonomy is Process, People, Organisation, Policy, Information, Technology, Experiment, and No action.
  - prompt: What must be recorded for each selected intervention?
    options:
      - Only a description
      - A description, a rationale, and whether it continues into a design workspace
      - Only whether it continues into a design workspace
      - A cost estimate and a delivery date
    answer: A description, a rationale, and whether it continues into a design workspace
    explanation: Each selected intervention independently records these three things.
  - prompt: What can the Shape Gate flag as a concern?
    options:
      - A technology intervention recorded without a rationale
      - Too many process interventions
      - A Domain Expert who has not answered within a day
      - An Initiative with more than five interventions
    answer: A technology intervention recorded without a rationale
    explanation: The Shape Gate can also flag that "no action" was never considered — both surface gaps without blocking the Facilitator.
