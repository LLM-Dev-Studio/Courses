# Module 1 Overview: What Is a Discovery Session

Before you run a session, you need the concepts behind it. This module
explains what an Initiative is, and who does what during Discover, Frame, and
Shape.

## Why This Matters

A Discovery Session is not a form to fill in. It is a structured
conversation. If you do not understand the record it builds and the roles
that build it, the session will feel arbitrary instead of purposeful.

## What You Will Cover

- What an Initiative is, and what it is not
- The Discover, Frame, and Shape journey
- The three roles: Facilitator, Domain Expert, and Agent Participant

## Where to Go

- [Initiatives](https://modeller.wiki/docs/concepts/initiatives) — the concepts
  covered in this module, in full
- [modeller.website](https://modeller.website) — where a Discovery Session
  actually runs

## Key Takeaway

An Initiative is one growing record of a change being explored — not a
separate document, and not a commitment to build software.
