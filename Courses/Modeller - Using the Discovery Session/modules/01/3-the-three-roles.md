# The Three Roles

A Discovery Session needs at least two people. A third, optional participant
can help with the workload.

## Facilitator

You guide the session. You:

- Curate which questions get asked, and in what order.
- Send questions to the Domain Expert.
- Decide what becomes a durable part of the record by accepting responses.
- Check the gates, choose interventions in Shape, and finalize the Initiative.

The Facilitator does the judgment work. Nothing becomes part of the record
without your acceptance.

## Domain Expert

The Domain Expert supplies knowledge about the situation. They:

- See only the change request and the one question currently waiting for
  them.
- Answer from what they know, in their own words.
- Do not see questions you are still drafting, gate findings, or the
  intervention comparison — that stays on your side of the session.

This narrow view keeps the Domain Expert focused on what they are actually
being asked, instead of the whole apparatus of the session.

## Agent Participant

An Agent Participant is optional. It can propose questions or candidate
interventions for people to accept or reject. It never makes a decision on
its own.

> [!NOTE]
> The core loop — a question followed by a Domain Expert response, drafted
> into the record by the Facilitator — works entirely without an Agent
> Participant. AI is a pluggable adapter, never a hard dependency.

## Key Takeaway

The Facilitator decides what enters the record, the Domain Expert supplies
the knowledge, and an optional Agent Participant can only propose — never
decide.
